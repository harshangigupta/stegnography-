Method and limitations

  1. Input images are kept in colour and resized to the cover dimensions for the experiment.
  2. Haar DWT is applied independently to R, G and B channels. The secret's LH and HL coefficients are embedded into the corresponding cover detail bands.
  3. Inverse DWT reconstructs each channel, which are combined into a colour stego image.
  4. A recovery key stores cover detail coefficients and the remaining secret coefficients needed by this demonstration's extraction procedure.
Important: this is a teaching demo, not a secure or blind steganography system. The recovery key is required and stores substantial extra data. Use the matching PNG and key; JPEG compression or editing may prevent correct extraction. Metrics compare the colour cover/stego images, while extraction metrics compare the recovered secret with the resized secret when both are available in the same session.
